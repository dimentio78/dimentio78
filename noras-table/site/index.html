04 · About

Cooked by Nora, in her own kitchen

Nora trained in restaurant kitchens in Lyon and Bologna, then came home to Tel Aviv and started cooking Friday dinners for her neighbours.

Today it is a small kitchen with two ovens and one rule: cook what you would serve at your own table. Pasta is rolled every morning, braises go on the night before, and nothing is reheated from yesterday.

Is the kitchen kosher?

The kitchen is not under supervision. Dairy and meat are cooked on separate days with separate equipment, and every dish is marked dairy, meat or pareve.

How far ahead should I book catering?

At least five days for up to 30 guests, and two weeks for larger events. Fridays fill up first.

Can you cook for allergies?

Yes, for most allergies. Add a note with your order or message Nora before you book, and she will tell you which dishes are safe.

How long does the food keep?

Two days in the fridge for mains and pasta, three days for cakes. Each box comes with reheating notes.
